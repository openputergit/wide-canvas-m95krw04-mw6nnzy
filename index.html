Our Story

Aappa Filter Coffee brings the authentic taste of South Indian filter coffee to Pune. Our journey began with a simple mission: to serve the perfect cup of filter coffee that reminds you of home.

Every cup is brewed with carefully selected coffee beans, following traditional methods that have been perfected over generations.
